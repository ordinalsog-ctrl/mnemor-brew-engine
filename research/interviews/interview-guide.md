# Interview Guide

## Goal

Find out whether premium home espresso users have a painful, frequent, paid problem around dial-in and repeatability.

Do not sell the idea during the first half of the interview.

## Target Interviewees

- La Marzocco Micra or Mini owners
- Acaia Lunar owners
- users who change beans regularly
- users who have tried coffee apps or manual logs

## Questions

1. Tell me about the last time you dialed in a new bean.
2. How many shots did it take until you were happy?
3. What did you change between shots?
4. Did you write anything down?
5. Have you ever tried to return to a previous good recipe?
6. What made that easy or hard?
7. Do you currently use any app, scale integration, notebook, or spreadsheet?
8. What made you keep using it or stop using it?
9. During a shot, where is your phone?
10. What part of the workflow feels annoying enough that you would pay to remove it?
11. If a device reduced failed dial-in shots, what would it need to prove before you trusted it?
12. At 149 EUR, would you buy, hesitate, or decline? Why?
13. At 199 EUR, what would need to be true?

## Evidence To Capture

- exact machine
- grinder
- scale
- beans per month
- shots per week
- current tracking behavior
- failed shots per new bean
- willingness to pay
- strongest quote
- objections

## Bad Questions

Avoid:

- "Would you like an AI espresso assistant?"
- "Is this interesting?"
- "Would you maybe use this?"

These produce politeness, not evidence.
