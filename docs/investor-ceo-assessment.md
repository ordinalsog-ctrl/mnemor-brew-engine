# Investor and CEO Assessment

## Current Verdict

As a VC-funded startup today: not investable.

As a bootstrapped premium niche product: worth testing.

As a long-term platform: possible only after strong evidence from a narrow product.

## Why It Is Not Yet Investable

The project does not yet have:

- proven demand
- proven willingness to pay
- a validated recommendation engine
- a defensible data asset
- a scalable acquisition channel
- evidence that the market is large enough for venture returns

The current opportunity is too narrow and too early for a venture-scale claim.

## Why It Is Still Interesting

The project has characteristics that can work for a small premium hardware/software company:

- technically reachable first prototype
- direct access to the target setup
- affluent enthusiast segment
- clear daily or weekly workflow
- strong community channels
- possible high unit margin if value is proven
- local-first positioning

## Core Risk

The biggest risk is not firmware.

The biggest risk is that the product becomes a well-built logger instead of a decision system.

If the user still has to interpret every flow curve and decide the next change manually, the product is weak.

## Core Opportunity

The opportunity is a controlled next-shot recommendation loop:

1. Capture shot.
2. Ask for minimal taste feedback.
3. Recommend one next change.
4. Observe whether it helped.
5. Learn setup-specific behavior.

## Competitive Risk

Existing coffee apps already cover shot tracking, Bluetooth scales, graphs, beans, water, and statistics. Hardware manufacturers can also move deeper into workflow automation.

Therefore, connectivity alone is not a moat.

## Potential Moat

The potential moat is not the number of stored shots.

The potential moat is a structured dataset of controlled espresso experiments:

- one variable changed at a time
- known setup
- known bean age
- measured result
- user preference outcome
- recommendation taken or ignored

That dataset can improve a recommendation model in ways generic shot logs cannot.

## CEO Rule

No expansion until evidence exists.

Do not add app, backend, universal scale support, industrial design, or partnership work until the first user-value proof is visible.
