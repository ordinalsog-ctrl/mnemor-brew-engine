# Product Hypotheses

Each hypothesis must be tested with evidence, not enthusiasm.

## H1 - Dial-In Pain Exists

Premium home espresso users waste meaningful time and beans when dialing in new beans.

Evidence needed:

- number of attempts per new bean
- grams wasted before acceptable shot
- frequency of bean changes
- emotional importance of repeatability

Pass signal:

- target users can recall recent failed dial-in sessions with specific details
- users describe the problem without being prompted

Fail signal:

- users enjoy manual trial and error enough that they do not want assistance

## H2 - Phone Friction Is Real

Users dislike opening, pairing, and managing a phone or tablet during espresso preparation.

Evidence needed:

- current use of tracking apps
- abandonment reasons
- bluetooth/app foreground pain
- preference for dedicated device

Pass signal:

- users say they stopped logging because it interrupted the workflow

Fail signal:

- users are happy with their existing phone app

## H3 - Automatic Capture Changes Behavior

If shot data is captured automatically, users will collect enough data for learning.

Evidence needed:

- shots captured per week
- manual corrections needed
- taste feedback completion rate

Pass signal:

- less than 5 seconds active interaction per shot
- taste feedback entered on at least 70% of shots

Fail signal:

- users ignore feedback input or disable capture

## H4 - Recommendations Beat Baseline Rules

A setup-specific recommendation engine can outperform simple espresso rules.

Evidence needed:

- baseline recommendation per shot
- engine recommendation per shot
- next-shot outcome
- user preference score

Pass signal:

- engine reaches target shot in fewer attempts than baseline

Fail signal:

- recommendations are obvious, generic, or inconsistent

## H5 - Users Pay For Outcome

Users will pay a premium device price if the system reduces failed shots and improves repeatability.

Evidence needed:

- preorder or reservation signal
- interview willingness to pay
- price sensitivity test
- retention after several weeks

Pass signal:

- at least 40% of external testers would pay 149 EUR or more

Fail signal:

- users like the idea but value it below 50 EUR
